INDIAN ANTI-CORRUPTION & PUBLIC RIGHTS REFERENCES

Know the law. Use official channels.

These are starting points for learning, not legal advice or an exhaustive list. Laws, commencement status, procedures and competent authorities can change; check the current official text and seek qualified advice for your situation.

  • Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988 (as amended): provisions addressing corruption offences and related procedures.
  • Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act, 2013: framework concerning the Lokpal and state Lokayuktas.
  • Right to Information Act, 2005: a statutory route to request information from public authorities, subject to the Act's rules and exemptions.
  • Whistle Blowers Protection Act, 2014: protection framework for disclosures; check its current commencement and applicable procedures before relying on it.
  • Constitution of India, Articles 14, 32 and 226: equality before law and constitutional remedies through the Supreme Court and High Courts, subject to legal requirements.

Official sources: India Code, Legislative Department, and Central Vigilance Commission.
